03 — Behind the bar From farm to final pour
Our method

Behind
the bar.

Four steps, repeated with unreasonable care. This is what "calibrated" means in practice.

  1. 01

    Source

    We buy direct from twelve farms and pay well above market. If we can't trace the lot, we don't pour it.

    Direct trade · 12 partners
  2. 02

    Roast

    Small batches, eleven kilos at a time, profiled on a vintage Probat and cupped every single morning.

    In-house · weekly
  3. 03

    Dial

    Refractometers out before service. Yield over twenty-one percent, always. We chase sweetness, not numbers — the numbers just help.

    Daily calibration · 6am
  4. 04

    Serve

    Weigh, tamp, time, taste. Then we do it again for the next person in line. Quality is a habit, not an accident.

    To order · always
